# Grandma’s Bakeria

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
First-time visitors playing a short bakery game on a phone, tablet, or desktop; returning players growing Grandma’s shop over five story days. Bakery owners can configure the same experience to introduce their business.

## Product Purpose
Help Grandma take orders, prepare pastries and drinks, and earn six regular customers so she can put away three fictional flyer runs and enjoy an hour for herself.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Quick Play, Story Mode, five interactive stations, recoverable mistakes, upgrades, daily recipe challenges, local saves, and a baking scrapbook. Keep sales and operating profit separate from fictional advertising savings. Real business links, offers, and featured products appear only when configured. Purchase rewards require verified integration and are hidden by default.

All artwork uses reusable HTML, CSS, inline SVG, and procedural animation. No required downloaded images, fonts, video, sprite sheets, stock art, or audio. At most one optional owner-provided logo. Core play needs no backend, external API, account, or paid service.

## Brand Commitments
The user explicitly requests a mobile-first visual direction that directly follows https://www.heytea.com/, including its typography, spacing, open white surfaces, and black line asset style, replacing the existing sketchbook design. Keep Grandma’s Bakeria original, charming, and warm; retain the story and complete gameplay requirements. Use the exact supplied introductory line in `src/business-config.js`.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing recipe, customer, scoring, progression, and business modules in `src/`; engine and configuration tests in `tests/`. No real bakery address, menu URLs, offers, or logo have been supplied.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
44px or larger important touch targets; touch, pointer, and keyboard alternatives; no required dragging or rotation; persistent tickets and oven alerts; pause, hidden-tab pause, sound controls, relaxed play, and reduced motion.

## Latest asset direction

The user supplied 17 original transparent PNGs and a Goodnotes character-sheet reference, explicitly superseding the initial zero-image restriction for these files. Keep their original appearance. Use static original character crops; remove redrawn characters, additional shading, 3D treatment and character animations. The core game remains interactive with explicit preparation progress.
